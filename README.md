# Papers

Papers by Daniel Boles on pseudocode as a specification language for AI work, and on detecting
statistical watermarks in text from large language models. Each paper is here as PDF and Markdown.

## Index

### Pseudocode as a Guardrail in Artificial Intelligence: Contracts, Benchmarks, and Cross-Language Verification

- **Date:** July 26, 2026
- **Abstract:** A single-case empirical study that builds and runs a contract benchmark, a live
  spec-first LLM guardrail, a cross-repository audit, a second compiler backend and teaching
  artifacts, and finds eight defects by execution and citation errors in nine of fourteen sources.
- **Read:** [PDF](pdf/pseudocode-as-a-guardrail-2026.pdf), [Markdown](md/pseudocode-as-a-guardrail-2026.md)
- **Code:** [pseudocode-ai-workflows](https://github.com/dboles99/pseudocode-ai-workflows)
- **Cite:** Boles, D. (2026). *Pseudocode as a guardrail in artificial intelligence: Contracts,
  benchmarks, and cross-language verification* [Manuscript]. https://github.com/dboles99/papers

### Pseudocode in Artificial Intelligence: Systemic Logic, Heuristics, and Workflow Generation

- **Date:** 2025
- **Abstract:** A review of fourteen sources arguing that pseudocode works as both an intermediary
  language and a structural map between human intent and AI behaviour, with runnable appendices.
- **Read:** [PDF](pdf/pseudocode-in-artificial-intelligence-2025.pdf), [Markdown](md/pseudocode-in-artificial-intelligence-2025.md)
- **Code:** [pseudocode-ai-workflows](https://github.com/dboles99/pseudocode-ai-workflows)
- **Cite:** Boles, D. (2025). *Pseudocode in artificial intelligence: Systemic logic, heuristics, and
  workflow generation* (Version 1.0.0) [Manuscript]. https://github.com/dboles99/papers
- **Reference note:** the reference list is kept as first written. The 2026 paper above audits it:
  nine identifiers were wrong and one source could not be located. Use the corrected list there.

### Detecting and Measuring Statistical Watermarks in Large Language Model Text: A Security-Oriented Evidence Synthesis and System Design

- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Abstract:** An evidence synthesis of 20 papers concluding that LLM text watermarks support keyed
  provenance under compatible, calibrated conditions but are not a universal test of authorship,
  with a system design that keeps each method separate and reports uncertainty.
- **Status:** design and evidence draft. Empirical validation of the application was pending at the
  date of the paper.
- **Read:** [PDF](pdf/detecting-and-measuring-llm-text-watermarks-2026.pdf), [Markdown](md/detecting-and-measuring-llm-text-watermarks-2026.md)
- **Code:** [llm-watermark-lab](https://github.com/dboles99/llm-watermark-lab)
- **Cite:** Boles, D. (2026). *Detecting and measuring statistical watermarks in large language model
  text: A security-oriented evidence synthesis and system design* [Manuscript].
  https://github.com/dboles99/papers

### A Cybersecurity Architecture for LLM Text Watermark Detection and Controlled Watermarking

- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Abstract:** A white paper recommending a research laboratory with four distinct findings,
  secure key handling, immutable run records and human review, and no generic "AI percentage".
- **Status:** design and evidence draft. Empirical validation of the application was pending at the
  date of the paper.
- **Read:** [PDF](pdf/llm-text-watermark-detection-white-paper-2026.pdf), [Markdown](md/llm-text-watermark-detection-white-paper-2026.md)
- **Code:** [llm-watermark-lab](https://github.com/dboles99/llm-watermark-lab)
- **Cite:** Boles, D. (2026). *A cybersecurity architecture for LLM text watermark detection and
  controlled watermarking* [White paper]. https://github.com/dboles99/papers

## 简体中文摘要

- **《作为护栏的伪代码》(2026):** 单案例实证研究。构建并实际运行合约基准、规格优先的大模型护栏、跨仓库审计、第二个编译器后端和教学材料,通过执行发现八个缺陷,并发现十四条引文中有九条标识符有误。
- **《人工智能中的伪代码》(2025):** 综述十四篇文献,论证伪代码既是中间语言,也是连接人类意图与人工智能行为的结构图。
- **《检测与度量大语言模型文本中的统计水印》(2026):** 综合二十篇论文的证据。结论是水印在兼容且经过校准的条件下可作为带密钥的来源证据,但不能普遍判定作者身份。
- **《大语言模型文本水印检测的网络安全架构》(2026):** 白皮书,建议建立研究实验室,区分四类结论,保护密钥,保留不可变的运行记录,并由人工审查,不输出笼统的“AI 百分比”。

## 繁體中文摘要

- **《作為護欄的虛擬碼》(2026):** 單一個案實證研究。建置並實際執行合約基準、規格優先的大型語言模型護欄、跨儲存庫稽核、第二個編譯器後端與教學材料,透過執行發現八項缺陷,並發現十四筆引文中有九筆識別碼有誤。
- **《人工智慧中的虛擬碼》(2025):** 回顧十四篇文獻,論證虛擬碼既是中介語言,也是連結人類意圖與人工智慧行為的結構圖。
- **《偵測與量測大型語言模型文字中的統計浮水印》(2026):** 彙整二十篇論文的證據。結論是浮水印在相容且經校準的條件下可作為以金鑰驗證的來源證據,但無法普遍判定作者身分。
- **《大型語言模型文字浮水印偵測的資安架構》(2026):** 白皮書,建議建立研究實驗室,區分四種結論,保護金鑰,保留不可變的執行紀錄,並由人工審查,不輸出籠統的「AI 百分比」。
